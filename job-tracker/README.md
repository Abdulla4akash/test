# EU–UK Tech Job Tracker

A single-page tracker for software, SDE, ML and AI job hunting across Europe and the UK.
Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step and no install needed.

## What it does

- **Pipeline**: Kanban board (Wishlist → Applied → OA / Screen → Interviews → Offer → Closed) plus a list view.
  Drag cards between columns, set next-step dates, and the "Next steps" panel flags anything late or due soon.
  Filter by track (SWE, SDE, ML Engineer, AI / LLM, Research Eng, Quant Dev and more) and by country.
- **Employers**: about 60 companies that hire engineers in the EU and UK (big tech, AI labs, scale-ups, quant firms),
  with offices, focus areas, careers links and a one-click "Track" button.
- **Job search**: pick a role and a city or country, and get ready-made searches for LinkedIn, Indeed, Google Jobs,
  Welcome to the Jungle, plus Google searches of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Personio and SmartRecruiters
  job pages. Also includes a yearly hiring calendar (quant and big-tech grad windows).
- **Visas**: the main work routes per country (UK Skilled Worker / Graduate / HPI / Global Talent, EU Blue Card,
  Dutch HSM and orientation year, Irish Critical Skills, and more), with links to the official pages and sponsor registers.

## Data

When opened as a local file, applications are saved in your browser's localStorage.
Use **Export CSV / Export JSON** to back them up and **Import JSON** to restore or move them.
The first run shows five example applications; remove them with **Remove examples**.

Visa rules and salary thresholds change every year. Always confirm on the official page linked in the Visas tab.
